## 2. English

**Short description (60 chars):**
Shared, transparent cashbook for trips and community events.

**Full description:**
```
Wargakas is a shared cashbook that keeps community money clear and trusted. Use it for trips, gatherings, weddings, neighbourhood events, study tours, or any group that collects and spends money together.

WHY WARGAKAS
Paper notes and retyped reports cause mistakes and suspicion. Wargakas gives everyone in the group the same, traceable record: who paid, what was spent, what is left.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Create an event with dates, budget and currency
- Add participants and track who has paid
- Record contributions and expenses in seconds
- Attach receipt photos as proof
- See the balance and totals on one summary screen
- Share a read-only link so participants can check the books without installing anything or creating an account
- Export reports as PDF or CSV, or share a WhatsApp-ready summary
- Send reminders to participants through WhatsApp, and get on-device reminder notifications
- Invite other committee members to help; the treasurer controls access
- See an audit trail of every change: who did what and when

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
- Works offline; your data syncs when you are back online
- 13 languages and 40+ currencies, including right-to-left Arabic
- Large text, high contrast, dark mode and a calm, simple layout designed for treasurers of all ages

PRIVACY FIRST
- No ads and no tracking
- We never sell your data
- Receipt photos are private to your event members
- Phone numbers are optional and never appear in reports or the public link
- The treasurer can revoke the share link at any time
- You can delete your account inside the app

IMPORTANT
Wargakas is a record-keeping tool. It is not a bank or payment service and does not hold or move money.

Questions or feedback: the Wargakas support page (https://github.com/yukimurakanzaki/eventagent/issues)
```

## 7. Account deletion requirement (Google Play)

Play requires (a) an in-app deletion path and (b) a **public web URL** where a user can request deletion without installing the app. In-app path exists (Account -> Hapus akun). Before submission:

1. Publish a simple page at https://github.com/yukimurakanzaki/eventagent/blob/main/event-planner-agent/docs/account-deletion.md (for example a section of the GitHub Pages site or your website) that states the app name "Wargakas" and operator, the steps to delete in-app, an email request option (the Wargakas support page (https://github.com/yukimurakanzaki/eventagent/issues), send from your registered address, subject "Delete my Wargakas account"), what is deleted and retained (copy from Privacy Policy section 6) and the time to complete (for example within 30 days).
2. Enter that URL in Play Console -> App content -> Data safety -> Delete account URL.
3. Be ready to process emailed requests manually: delete the user in Supabase Authentication (after checking treasurer handover) or run the equivalent of `delete_my_account()` on their behalf.

Apple also requires in-app deletion for apps with account creation: satisfied by Account -> Hapus akun.
