# Delete your Wargakas account

You can delete your Wargakas account and its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Wargakas and sign in.
2. Tap the **account icon** (top right) → **Delete account** ("Hapus akun").
3. Tick the confirmation box and tap **Delete account**.

What happens:

- Your sign-in (email and password) is deleted immediately.
- If you are the treasurer and other organisers belong to your group, the treasurer role passes to one of them and the group's cashbook history stays available to the group without your account name.
- If nobody else belongs to your group, its events, cashbook history, share links and uploaded receipt photos are deleted.
- Cached data and receipt photos on your phone are removed.
- Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within its normal backup retention period.

Download a PDF or CSV report first if you want to keep a copy.

## Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, open a request on our support page with the subject **"Delete my account"** and the email address of the account:

https://github.com/yukimurakanzaki/eventagent/issues

Do not post your password. We confirm ownership by sending a message to the account email, then delete the account within 30 days.
